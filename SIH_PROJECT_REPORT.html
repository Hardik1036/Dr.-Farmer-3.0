🌱 Dr. Farmer (AgriVision)

Full-Stack AI-Powered Crop & Livestock Disease Advisory Platform
Domain: Agriculture & Rural Development / AI & ML
Target Audience: Indian Farmers, Rural Agronomists, Vets
Live Backend API: https://dr-farmer-3-0.onrender.com
Cloud Database: Supabase Cloud PostgreSQL (with RLS)
Repository: github.com/b25bs1115-eng/Dr.-Farmer-3.0
Frontend: React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS (Vercel)

1. Executive Summary

Agriculture supports over 58% of India's rural population, yet crop blights and livestock epidemics cause annual yield losses exceeding ₹1.5 lakh crore. Smallholder farmers face severe bottlenecks including delayed physical diagnosis by scarce agricultural officers, literacy barriers on scientific portals, and panic-driven chemical pesticide overuse.

Dr. Farmer is an AI-powered, mobile-first advisory platform engineered for grassroots Indian agriculture. A farmer simply captures a photo of an infected leaf or cattle symptom to receive instant (3-second) disease diagnosis, severity evaluation, and a dual-action treatment plan (organic home remedies + veterinary/chemical prescriptions) with full multilingual spoken voice narration.

2. Problem Statement & Challenges Addressed

⏳ Diagnostic Delays

Physical agronomy visits take 5–15 days, allowing fungal blights to spread rapidly across entire farms.

📖 Literacy & Language Barrier

Over 40% of rural farmers struggle with complex scientific manuals and English-heavy interfaces.

🧪 Chemical & Financial Overuse

Lack of organic alternative guidance leads to excessive chemical pesticide purchasing and soil degradation.

🐄 Cattle Epidemics (LSD/FMD)

Livestock diseases like Lumpy Skin Disease go undetected until herd-wide contagion occurs.

3. Key Innovations & Platform Features

  • Universal Point-and-Shoot AI Scan: Identifies 38 distinct crop diseases across Tomato, Potato, Corn, Grape, Apple, Peach, Bell Pepper, Strawberry, and cattle conditions like Lumpy Skin Disease (LSD).
  • Dual-Action Advisory Model: Delivers low-cost Organic Home Remedies (buttermilk spray, neem emulsion, ash dusting) alongside Medical/Veterinary Prescriptions (Mancozeb, Metalaxyl, bactericides).
  • Spoken Voice Narration (Web Speech API): Low-literacy farmers can listen to audio advice in Hindi, English, Bengali, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Gujarati, and Punjabi.
  • Digital Farm Record Book: Cloud-synchronized tracking of Kharif/Rabi/Zaid crop cycles, sowing dates, expected harvest, pesticide safety windows, and livestock vaccination due dates.
  • 1-Tap Emergency Expert Hotline: Direct dialer connecting farmers instantly to the Kisan Call Center (1800-180-1551) for urgent disease alerts.
  • Resilient Cloud Architecture: Supabase cloud database with automatic offline graceful fallback ensuring zero downtime in remote rural regions.

Voice API HTML5 Web Speech Synthesis & Recognition Native browser audio synthesis requiring 0KB extra npm dependencies.

Database Supabase (PostgreSQL with RLS) Real-time synchronization, secure row-level policies, and instant querying.
Deployment Vercel (Frontend Edge CDN), Render (Backend) Global high availability, zero maintenance, scalable serverless setup.

6. Machine Learning Optimization & Quantization

Trained on the PlantVillage dataset (54,305 high-resolution labeled images) and curated livestock dermatological datasets. The neural networks were optimized for production deployment:

  • Model FlatBuffers Conversion: Full Keras .keras models (17.6 MB) were converted to .tflite format (4.5 MB), eliminating training-only optimizers and computation graphs.
  • LiteRT Inference Runtime: Replaced the heavy ~1GB TensorFlow library with the lightweight ~5MB ai-edge-litert runtime, preventing cloud memory crashes and enabling instant server boot.
  • Prediction Latency: Executes full image preprocessing, tensor forward pass, and class mapping in under 45 milliseconds.

7. Database Design (Supabase PostgreSQL)

1. disease_catalog

Stores 38 plant classes & cattle health entries, Hindi titles, severity levels, medical treatments, and organic remedies.

2. diagnostic_logs

Live telemetry logging each farmer's scan ID, detected disease, confidence score, and timestamp for epidemic tracking.

3. crop_cycles

Farmer's digital ledger tracking crop name, season (Kharif/Rabi), sowing date, harvest milestones, and pesticide safety.

4. livestock_profiles

Animal tags, species profiles, last vaccination dates, and automated next-due-date reminders.
